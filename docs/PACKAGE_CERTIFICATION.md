## Resources without a generator in the repository

Every resource above except the following is written by a script that is committed alongside it:

- manuscript: `manuscript/figures/fig15_mechanism_iso.pdf`

`fig15_mechanism_iso.pdf` (Main Fig. 4) is rendered from the steady velocity fields of the stationary N=512 campaign (uniform lid, k=1, k=2). Those fields are raw dumps of about 16 MB per control, so they are held outside the git tree with the other raw fields and are supplied from the corresponding author on request; the Data availability statement of the manuscript says so. The dissipation values that the same section quotes for those three controls do have committed records, `results/p1_gci_iso/p1_gci_raw.csv` (read by `scripts/make_fig_gci.py`) and `results/p1_gci_iso/p1_gci_summary.csv` (read by `scripts/export_legacy_supp_tables.py`). The strain-rate maximum and the interior strain means of panel (c) are read off those field files and are not recomputed by any committed script.
